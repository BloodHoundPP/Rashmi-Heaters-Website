Design a modern, professional, and premium B2B industrial website for a company named "Rashmi Heaters", which manufactures industrial heating solutions.

GOAL:
Transform the brand perception from a local manufacturer to a global, high-quality industrial engineering company. The design should feel trustworthy, precise, and technically advanced, similar to global brands like Siemens or Bosch.

STYLE DIRECTION:
Use an “Industrial Premium” design style:

* Dark theme with charcoal, deep blue, and steel gray tones
* Accent color: bright orange or electric blue for CTAs
* Clean, grid-based layout with strong alignment
* Minimal but impactful use of shadows and depth
* Sharp, modern typography (Inter or Poppins)
* High contrast, bold headings
* Use subtle micro-interactions and hover effects

LAYOUT:
Use a 12-column grid system (max width ~1200–1320px). Maintain consistent spacing using an 8px spacing system. Ensure strong visual hierarchy and whitespace.

HOMEPAGE STRUCTURE:

1. HERO SECTION:

* Split layout (left text, right image/visual)
* Headline: “Precision Industrial Heating Solutions”
* Subtext: “High-performance heaters engineered for reliability and efficiency across industries.”
* Primary CTA: “Explore Products”
* Secondary CTA: “Get a Quote”
* Include trust badges (ISO certification) and client logos
* Background can include subtle industrial textures or gradients

2. TRUST INDICATORS STRIP:

* Horizontal section with 4 metrics:

  * 20+ Years Experience
  * 500+ Clients
  * 1000+ Installations
  * ISO Certified
* Use icons + bold numbers

3. PRODUCT CATEGORIES:

* Grid of 4–6 cards
* Each card includes product image, title, short description, and “View Details” CTA
* Add hover effect (scale + shadow)

4. INDUSTRIES SERVED:

* Icon-based grid layout
* Include industries like Plastic, Pharma, Packaging, Automotive
* Clean cards with subtle hover animations

5. WHY CHOOSE US:

* Split layout
* Left: industrial image or factory visual
* Right: bullet points:

  * High durability
  * Custom manufacturing
  * Fast delivery
  * Technical support

6. FEATURED PRODUCTS:

* Horizontal scroll or grid
* Each card includes image, short specs, and “Download Datasheet”

7. PROCESS SECTION:

* Horizontal timeline showing:

  1. Requirement Analysis
  2. Design
  3. Manufacturing
  4. Testing
  5. Delivery

8. TESTIMONIALS / CLIENTS:

* Display client logos or testimonial cards

9. CTA BANNER:

* Full-width section
* Text: “Need a custom heating solution?”
* Button: “Get a Quote”

10. FOOTER:

* Multi-column layout with:

  * Products
  * Industries
  * Company
  * Contact info
  * Social links

INNER PAGES:

PRODUCT LIST PAGE:

* Grid layout with filters (type, wattage, application)
* Clean sidebar filters
* Product cards with quick specs

PRODUCT DETAIL PAGE:

* Large product images
* Technical specifications table
* Applications section
* Download datasheet button
* Inquiry form

ABOUT PAGE:

* Company overview
* Factory images
* Certifications
* Timeline

CONTACT PAGE:

* Contact form
* Embedded map
* Phone, email, address
* Floating WhatsApp CTA

COMPONENT SYSTEM:
Create reusable components:

* Navbar (sticky, transparent to solid on scroll)
* Buttons (primary, secondary, outline)
* Cards (product, industry, testimonial)
* Section headers
* Forms and input fields
* Tables for technical specs

UI DETAILS:

* Smooth hover animations
* Sticky header
* Consistent icon style (line icons)
* Subtle gradients and overlays
* Clean spacing and alignment

ADVANCED TOUCHES:

* Add a floating WhatsApp “Talk to Expert” button
* Include a modern product showcase feel (3D or angled product images)
* Use subtle scroll-based animations for sections

OUTPUT:
Design a complete high-fidelity homepage and key inner pages with a consistent design system, modern UI components, and professional industrial branding.
